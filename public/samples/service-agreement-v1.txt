SAMPLE SERVICE AGREEMENT — VERSION 1
For demonstration only. This is fictional and is not a contract for real use.

Parties: Acme Design Studio (Client) and Riya Shah (Consultant).

1. Services. The Consultant will deliver a website accessibility review and written report to the Client by 30 October 2026.

2. Payment. The Client will pay the Consultant INR 10,000 within 30 days after receiving an invoice. The agreement does not specify a late-payment penalty.

3. Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement by giving the other party 30 days' written notice.

4. Confidentiality. Both parties must keep non-public project information confidential during the agreement and for two years after termination.

5. Ownership. After full payment, the Client owns the final written report. The Consultant may retain pre-existing tools and templates.

6. Disputes. The parties will first try to resolve a dispute through discussion. The agreement does not specify a court or arbitration process.
